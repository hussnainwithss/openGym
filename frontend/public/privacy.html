Privacy

Last changed: 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • This is a private, self-hosted openGym server. It is not run by a company and has no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Your training log and anything you connect to it are stored on this server only, on hardware its operator controls. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • WHOOP is only connected if you connect it yourself, and only reads from WHOOP. Nothing is sent to WHOOP.
  • You can disconnect WHOOP or delete your account at any time.

What this server stores

  • Your account: a display name, your passkeys (public keys only) and, if switched on, a password hash.
  • Your training log: workouts, plans, body weight, measurements, notes, photos and videos you add, and your settings. Your own devices keep a copy so the app works offline.
  • An activity log of sign-ins and account changes, with the address the request came from, so the operator can spot misuse.

WHOOP

If you choose Settings → Integrations → WHOOP → Connect and allow it on WHOOP's own page, this server gets read access to your WHOOP data with these permissions: recovery, cycles, workouts, sleep, profile, body measurement, and offline (so it can keep reading without asking you to sign in again).

What it keeps:

  • Your WHOOP workouts, as activities in your training log (sport, time, length, distance, strain, heart rate, energy).
  • Your weight, when it changes in your WHOOP profile, as a weigh-in.
  • Day by day: recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, sleep, strain and steps, with your full WHOOP history from the day you connect.
  • Your WHOOP user id, to know which WHOOP account the data belongs to.
  • The sign-in WHOOP gives this server, encrypted on disk.

It reads your WHOOP profile when you connect only to learn your WHOOP user id. Your name and email from WHOOP are not stored.

How it is used: only to show you this data in openGym (your activities, weigh-ins, the Daily form and the WHOOP page). It is not used for anything else, not combined with anyone else's data, and not given to anyone.

Who else is involved

  • WHOOP sends the data to this server when it asks, and may tell this server that something changed. WHOOP's own privacy policy covers what WHOOP does.
  • Cloudflare carries the connection to this server (a Cloudflare tunnel). It handles the traffic, as any network provider does, but this server does not use it to store your data.
  • Nobody else. No analytics, advertising or data brokers.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • Disconnect WHOOP (Settings → Integrations → WHOOP): this server forgets the WHOOP sign-in and stops reading. What was already imported stays in your log until you delete it.
  • Remove this server's access at WHOOP too: in the WHOOP app's settings, under the apps connected to your account.
  • Delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account): your account, training log, uploads, WHOOP sign-in and WHOOP history are deleted from this server. Lines about your account in the activity log stay until the log is cleared.
  • Backups the operator keeps of this server may hold your data until they are replaced.

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses passkeys. Integration sign-ins are encrypted on disk, and the data files are readable only by the server.

Questions
